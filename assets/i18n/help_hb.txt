{{app_name}} - yer music stash wrangler an' player

GITTIN' STARTED:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Music Stash → Toss In a Folder: pick a folder o' music; it gits looked over into the catalog.
  Toss in as many folders / spots as ya like.
• Music Stash → Wrangle Folders: see ever watched folder with its song count; add,
  point at a moved drive, look again, or chuck one out. Chuckin' one takes its songs outta the
  stash but never deletes files off the disk.
• Music Stash → Look Again at Everthang: look over watched folders to pick up new or changed files.
• Yer stash is a li'l SQLite database. Song name, who sung it, record, kind, year,
  track number an' tucked-in album pitcher git read from ever file.

PLAYIN' MUSIC:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Double-click any song to start playin' from there on through the rest o' the list.
• Transport (bottom bar): Back One, Play/Hold On, Whoa, On to the Next.
• Pickin' & Grinnin' menu: Mix 'Em Up plays the line in a scrambled order; Do It Again → Quit
  After / Loop the Whole Mess (loop the line) / Loop This'n (loop the song playin'). Both git
  remembered. Skippin' with Next/Back always moves on, even with Loop This'n.
• Pickin' & Grinnin' → ReplayGain: when on, songs tagged with ReplayGain play at a steady
  loudness (peak-limited so boosted songs don't clip). Off to start. "Loudness: Go by the Album"
  evens out by album loudness (keepin' an album's own quiet/loud balance) when album tags are
  there, else it falls back to track gain. To tag files yerself, mark 'em an' use Music Stash →
  Measure Loudness o' Marked… (or right-click → Scan ReplayGain): the app measures loudness
  (BS.1770 / ReplayGain 2.0) an' writes track + album ReplayGain tags into MP3/FLAC.
• Pickin' & Grinnin' → Tone Knobs…: a 10-band graphic equalizer (31 Hz – 16 kHz, ±12 dB) that
  shapes the tone live. Tick Enable, drag the band sliders, or pick a preset (Flat, Bass Boost,
  Vocal, Rock, …). Save yer own settin's with "Save preset…" an' chuck 'em with "Delete preset".
  Reset (Flat) zeroes the bands; turnin' it off is plumb transparent.
• Pickin' & Grinnin' → 'Tween Songs: Quiet Bit (usual), No Gap (songs butt up with no silence),
  or Fade Into It (the goin'-out song fades out as the next'n fades in).
  No Gap/Fade Into It are still bein' tinkered with.
• Show What's Comin' (Pickin' & Grinnin' → Show What's Comin'): see what's lined up in play order,
  with the song playin' marked ▶. Right-click a song → Add to the Line (or Music Stash → Add
  Marked / Add All Showin' to the Line) tacks 'em on without buttin' in on what's playin'.
  In the line winder: Play the picked song, Chuck it, move it Up/Down, or Clear It All.
• Hover a toolbar or transport button to see a li'l note explainin' what it does.
• Position bar shows time gone by / total time — drag it to jump around in a song.
• Two loudness knobs sit bottom right:
  - "App" sets just this player's own level (remembered ever launch).
  - "Sys" drives yer 'puter's main output volume, with a hush toggle beside it. It stays in
    step with the system level — includin' changes ya make with the volume keys or the OS mixer
    — an' moves the whole machine's volume, not just this app.
• "Bal" sets the left/right stereo balance (remembered ever launch). It's got a soft middle
  catch, an' the "C" button beside it snaps back to dead-center.
  Handy when the speakers ain't set up even.
• The picked-row marker follows the song that's playin' now.

STARS & PLAY COUNTIN':
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Stars are by hand: set a 1–5 star ratin' yerself. Times played gits counted separate (its own
  column) an' is NOT turned into stars.
• Click the stars in a song's Stars column to set a ratin'; click the same star again to wipe it
  (back to no stars). The "Stars:" buttons (bottom) an' the right-click menu do the same. All
  five stars are clickable.
• Lookin' → Count a Play After: pick how much o' a song's gotta play afore its play count goes
  up (25 / 50 / 75 / 90% or Plumb to the End). Skippin' never counts.
• Tip: sift by Times Played (column sift) to find, say, everthang ya played 5 times, then star
  them songs however ya like.

FINDIN' & SORTIN' OUT:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Hunt box (top): sifts by song name, who sung it, record, kind or file name as ya type.
• Column sifts (Lookin' → Show the Filter Boxes): a row o' per-column boxes — type in Song Name,
  Who Sung It, Record, Kind to match a piece; Year an' Times Played take a pattern (e.g. Year
  202[456] matches 2024-2026; Times Played 5 = played exactly five times, 0 = never played). They
  team up with each other an' the hunt/Show sift. Wipe 'em with the ✕ button; hidin' the row wipes
  'em too.
• "Show" sift (top): All the Songs, No Stars Yet, exact stars, or N-stars-an'-up.
• Click a column header to sort; click again to flip it (the Pitcher column don't sort).
• Lookin' menu: show the Length, Format, Bitrate, Kind, Track # an' File Name columns if ya like
  (Length's on to start, the rest off; yer picks git remembered). Length an' Bitrate fill in
  shortly after launch/scan as the app reads ever song's play time in the background, so they may
  be blank fer a moment.
• Jump to the song playin': click its name in the bottom bar, or use the toolbar "Now Playin'"
  button (next to Look Again). Handy after ya scrolled, hunted, or sorted away. (It only finds the
  song — it don't start or stop playin'.)
• Picks up where ya left off: the app remembers yer sort order an', on relaunch, picks an' scrolls
  to the song ya was last playin' (it don't auto-play).
• Copy songs to another spot: Lookin' → Markin' Checkboxes adds a ✓ column; tick rows (click the
  ✓ header to flip all showin'), or Music Stash → Mark All Showin'. Then Music Stash → Copy Marked
  To… lets ya pick Flat or Sort into Who-Sung-It/Record folders, then a spot - e.g. sift to 5
  stars, mark all, copy to a USB stick. A progress box shows ever file an' lets ya Quit it.
  (Name clashes git a " (2)" tacked on; nothin' gits writ over.)
• Right-click a song fer Play, stars, Stick On a Pitcher (local image file or web link, kept in
  the stash), Show in yer file manager, an' Show the Whole Path (with a Copy button).
• Right-click Rename the File… / Fix the Tags… / Give Stars normally work on just that one song.
  But if ya right-click a song that's marked (✓), they work on the whole marked bunch instead —
  the menu says which (e.g. "Fix Tags on 5 marked…", "Give Stars (5 marked)").
• Rename the File… (right-click) renames the file on disk from its tags usin' a pattern o' tokens
  — %track% %title% %artist% %album% %albumartist% %year% %genre% — e.g. "%track% %title% -
  %artist%". A live peek shows the new name afore ya do it; the extension's always kept an' the
  catalog updates right in place (no lookin' again). %track% gits zero-padded to two digits.
• Fix the Tags… (right-click) opens an editor fer song name, who sung it, record, album artist,
  year, track #, kind, comment, an' the tucked-in cover pitcher. Changes git writ back into the
  file fer MP3 an' FLAC; OGG an' WAV open read-only fer now. Tags from filename… (in that editor)
  reads the file's name into the fields usin' the same token pattern, fer ya to look over afore
  savin' — it never writes on its own. If ya fix the song that's playin' right now, playin' stops
  first so the file can be saved. The catalog (an' any thumbnail) updates straight away.
• Fix tags on a bunch at once: mark songs (Markin' Checkboxes / Mark All Showin'), then Music
  Stash → Fix Tags on Marked… Ya can mix four kinds o' change in one pass: (1) From filename — set
  song name/who-sung-it/track #/etc. on ever song by readin' its name with a token pattern (the
  bulk form o' Tags from filename; files that don't match git left be); (2) Set fields — tick Who
  Sung It, Record, Album Artist, Kind, Year or Comment to give 'em one value across the whole
  bunch (these beat the pattern); (3) Find & replace — change text within one field across the
  bunch (e.g. "feat."→"ft.", ignore case if ya like); (4) Cover pitcher — set one image as the
  cover fer all, or yank the cover off all. A progress box shows ever file with a Quit button;
  OGG/WAV git skipped.
• Rename a bunch at once: mark songs, then Music Stash → Rename Marked by Pattern… A peek list
  shows ever old → new name; doin' it renames 'em all (name clashes git a " (2)" tacked on,
  nothin' gits writ over) with a Quit button.
• Stash Rundown (Music Stash → Stash Rundown…): sum up yer collection grouped by Who Sung It,
  Record, or Kind; in layers (Who Sung It → Record, Record → Who Sung It, Kind → Who Sung It); or
  as a flat song list. Tick "List tracks" to open up ever group an' show the songs in it. Sort by
  Name or Year with an up/down toggle, switch between the whole stash an' the current sifted view,
  an' Export CSV… to save the rundown. Pick "Double-Ups" to sniff out songs ya got more'n
  once, matched by Who Sung It + Song Name, Who Sung It + Song Name + Record, or File Name; ever
  bunch lists every copy with its whole path an' play count so ya can pick which'ns to keep.

SONG LISTS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Static song lists (Song Lists menu): Save This List… writes the songs yer lookin' at to a
  totin'-around .m3u8 file, with paths relative to the file so a list an' its music travel
  together (e.g. on a USB stick). Open a List… loads one an' plays it; files still in yer catalog
  keep their stars/play counts.
• Smart lists (Song Lists → New Smart List…): save sift rules — a ratin', an' maybe Kind / Who
  Sung It / Record (partial, don't mind the case — e.g. "Wickham" matches "Phil Wickham"), plus
  free-text Hunt — under a name. Pickin' it from the Song Lists menu re-applies the rules live
  (the status bar shows ♫ <name>). They update on their own as yer stash changes. Wrangle Smart
  Lists… lets ya fix a saved list's rules (the pencil button, which can rename it too) or chuck
  ones ya don't want no more.

WINDERS & SYSTEM TRAY:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• A system-tray icon + menu mirrors the controls: Show/Hide all winders, Play/Hold On, Back One,
  On to the Next, Whoa, Skedaddle.
• Hide It All tucks the app away an' holds playin'; Show It All brings it back (press Play to git
  goin' again).
• The main winder remembers its size betwixt launches.

TOTIN'-AROUND STASH (movin' betwixt machines or drives):
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Song spots are kept relative to each watched folder, so if a totin'-around drive shows up as
  D:\ on one PC an' E:\ on another, just use Music Stash → Point at a Moved Drive to re-point the
  folder — ever song under it follows along, keepin' its play counts an' stars.
• The database spot can be set with the -db command-line flag, the KBMP_DB environment variable,
  or Lookin' → Yer Druthers → Where the Stash Lives (restart to take hold); to start it sits next
  to the app (handy on a USB stick).

YER DRUTHERS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Lookin' → Yer Druthers… (Alt+R) gathers the common settin's in one spot: look, lingo,
  "count a play after" threshold, play loudness (remembered ever launch), whether to open the
  tone knobs at startup, the extra columns, an' where the stash lives. The same toggles stay on
  the Lookin' menu fer quick one-off changes.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Alt+P — Play / Hold On
• Alt+→ — Next song   • Alt+← — Song afore
• Alt+H — Hide all winders (an' hold). No shortcut to show again, on purpose —
  use the system tray or Lookin' → Show It All.
• Alt+R — Yer Druthers
• Cmd/Ctrl+Q — Skedaddle   • Cmd/Ctrl+W — Close winder   • Cmd/Ctrl+M — Tuck Down
